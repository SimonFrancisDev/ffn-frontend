# Fin Freedom Network Video Storyboard

## Format

- Recommended duration: 6–8 minutes
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 master, with a 9:16 social edit
- Style: clear motion graphics using the production website’s navy, blue, teal, green, purple, and gold palette
- Voice: calm, factual, simple, and confident

## Scene 1 — The network

**Visual:** Official logo over the production site’s deep navy background.

**Narration:** “Fin Freedom Network is a smart-contract-powered ecosystem built around participation, transparent rules, and verifiable on-chain activity.”

**On-screen text:** “Structure. Participation. Transparency.”

## Scene 2 — Ten levels, three engines

**Visual:** Levels 1 through 10 rise as a vertical ladder. P4, P12, and P39 labels repeat beside their assigned levels.

**Narration:** “The F-Freedom Program has ten levels. P4 powers Levels 1, 4, 7, and 10. P12 powers Levels 2, 5, and 8. P39 powers Levels 3, 6, and 9.”

## Scene 3 — Sponsor and matrix parent

**Visual:** A person registers through a sponsor. A second line shows where the person is structurally placed.

**Narration:** “A sponsor is the permanent referral relationship created at registration. A matrix parent is the person directly above a participant in a specific level’s orbit. They may be the same, but matrix placement can make them different.”

## Scene 4 — P4

**Visual:** Animate four positions filling from 1 to 4.

**Narration:** “P4 has one line and four positions. Ninety percent is the participant-side allocation and ten percent is the system charge. The fourth qualifying arrival completes the cycle, and the next cycle begins.”

## Scene 5 — P12 line 1

**Visual:** Show an owner, positions 1–3, and a new participant entering position 3.

**Narration:** “P12 has a forty percent component, a fifty percent component, and a ten percent system charge. On line 1, the owner receives forty percent and the eligible structural upline receives fifty percent.”

## Scene 6 — P12 line 2

**Visual:** Expand branches 4–7–10, 5–8–11, and 6–9–12. Place a participant at position 9 under position 3.

**Narration:** “On line 2, the orbit owner receives or allocates the fifty percent component, while the parent of that branch receives forty percent. Position 9, for example, belongs under position 3.”

## Scene 7 — One activation, two views

**Visual:** Use activation ID 561 from the audited example.

**Narration:** “A single activation can appear in more than one orbit. The first position is the original level activation. A second position records a routed payment received by another orbit owner. Both positions carry the same activation ID, so the chain proves they came from one transaction.”

**Example:** At Level 5, a 160 USDT activation paid 64 USDT to the first orbit owner, routed 80 USDT to the next eligible structural recipient, and assigned 16 USDT as the system charge.

## Scene 8 — P39

**Visual:** Reveal line 1, then line 2, then line 3.

**Narration:** “P39 has thirty-nine positions and three separate participant payment roles: twenty percent, twenty percent, and fifty percent. These roles must remain separate. The participant’s position determines who receives each component.”

## Scene 9 — P39 example

**Visual:** Show a participant at position 3, then position 12 under position 3, then position 37 under position 10.

**Narration:** “In this Level 3 example, the first recipient received 8 USDT, the next received 8 USDT, the next received 20 USDT, and 4 USDT went to the system. Every payment was represented in the correct structural line.”

## Scene 10 — Eligibility

**Visual:** An inactive wallet turns grey and is skipped; the next active wallet turns green.

**Narration:** “A participant can receive a payment component only when that exact level is active. If the selected wallet is inactive, the contract searches upward through the approved sponsor chain. The first eligible wallet receives the component. ID1 is used only when no eligible participant exists.”

## Scene 11 — Escrow and automatic upgrade

**Visual:** Gold amounts accumulate in a locked meter. The meter reaches its target and opens the next level.

**Narration:** “During the first cycle, selected amounts may be held in escrow toward the next level. The next level activates only when the exact requirement is reached. The same escrow cannot be used twice.”

## Scene 12 — Recycle

**Visual:** The final two qualifying arrivals fill a two-part reserve. The completed orbit moves into history and a new cycle opens.

**Narration:** “In P12 and P39, the final two qualifying arrivals build the recycle amount. Recycle is a repurchase. It closes the completed chapter and starts a new one through the participant’s permanent sponsor route.”

## Scene 13 — Self-payment protection

**Visual:** A payment arrow aimed back at the recycle owner is redirected to ID1.

**Narration:** “A participant cannot earn from their own repurchase. If a recycle component points back to the recycle owner, that component falls back to ID1 without manufacturing an orbit position.”

## Scene 14 — Verification

**Visual:** Show transaction hash, activation ID, level, line, position, receiver, amount, escrow, and cycle.

**Narration:** “Every activity can be verified through its transaction, activation ID, orbit, line, position, payment receipt, and resulting state.”

## Closing

**Visual:** Official logo and website.

**Narration:** “Fin Freedom Network. One Network. One Community. One Freedom. Together, we build freedom.”

**Required footer:** “Participation does not guarantee earnings. Outcomes depend on qualifying activity and smart-contract rules.”
